# AquaFlow

1. **Admin**
2. **Production Manager**
3. **Sales Manager**
4. **Customer**

### **1. Admin**

**Main Role:** Oversees and controls all system operations.
**Responsibilities:**

* Manage user accounts (add, edit, or remove staff and customers).
* Monitor system activities across departments.
* Access all reports — production, sales, inventory, and payments.
* Approve and track financial transactions.
* Ensure synchronization between modules (sales ↔ inventory ↔ production).
* Generate management-level insights and performance evaluations.

**Typical Interfaces:**

* Dashboard showing key metrics (production rate, sales, inventory levels).
* User management panel.
* Report generation and data export tools.

---

### **2. Production Manager**

**Main Role:** Controls production and stock levels.
**Responsibilities:**

* Record daily production (bottled water and beverage quantities).
* Update stock data to synchronize with sales and inventory.
* Track material usage and wastage.
* View and act upon production reports and trends.
* Receive alerts for low material or high demand.
* Coordinate with sales manager when production adjustments are needed.

**Typical Interfaces:**

* Production dashboard (input/output records).
* Inventory linkage panel (automatic update after production).
* Material usage log.

---

### **3. Sales Manager**

**Main Role:** Manages orders, sales transactions, and customer relationships.
**Responsibilities:**

* Receive and process customer orders.
* Approve or reject orders based on availability.
* Update payment status after verification.
* Ensure that inventory and production reflect sales changes.
* Generate daily, weekly, or monthly sales reports.
* Track performance (top customers, top-selling products).

**Typical Interfaces:**

* Sales order management panel.
* Payment confirmation interface.
* Customer database and communication tools.
* Sales reports and analytics.

---

### **4. Customer**

**Main Role:** End-user who places and tracks orders.
**Responsibilities:**

* View available products, sizes, and pricing in real-time.
* Place, cancel, or modify orders (within policy limits).
* Make digital payments via integrated gateways.
* Receive automated receipts and order confirmations.
* Track delivery status.
* Access their purchase history and invoices.

**Typical Interfaces:**

* Product catalog and order form.
* Payment gateway integration.
* Order tracking dashboard.
